# Quick Start Guide — Dynamic Pricing Sandbox

Welcome to the **Dynamic Pricing Sandbox**, an interactive simulator where you practice dynamic pricing across real-world-inspired scenarios. This guide walks you through everything you need to get started.

---

## 1. Sign In and Launch

1. Sign in with your account on the login page.
2. From the **Home** screen, click **Launch** to open the pricing simulator.

---

## 2. Choose a Scenario

The simulator includes four industry scenarios, unlocked sequentially:

| # | Scenario | Industry | Elasticity | Inventory | Unlock Requirement |
|---|----------|----------|------------|-----------|-------------------|
| 1 | 🛒 E-Commerce — Holiday Flash Sale | Retail | 1.4 (High) | 200 units | Available by default |
| 2 | ✈️ Airline Seats — Departure Countdown | Travel | 1.1 (Medium) | 90 seats | Score ≥ 60% on Scenario 1 |
| 3 | 🏨 Hotel Rooms — Convention Weekend | Hospitality | 0.9 (Low) | 60 rooms | Score ≥ 60% on Scenario 2 |
| 4 | 🎵 Concert Tickets — Multi-Tier Launch | Entertainment | 1.2 (High) | 500 tickets | Score ≥ 60% on Scenario 3 |

Select a scenario from the level-select screen to proceed to the briefing.

---

## 3. Read the Briefing

Before each scenario, you'll see a **Briefing** page containing:

- **Situation Overview** — Background and context for the pricing challenge.
- **Key Metrics** — Base price, price range, inventory, elasticity, optimal revenue, and number of rounds.
- **Competitor Intelligence** — What your competitor is expected to do with their pricing.
- **Strategic Hints** — Tips to guide your approach.
- **Available Tools** — Discounts, promotions, and bundles you can use during gameplay.

Read this carefully — it contains the information you need to form a strategy.

## 4. Pick Your Simulation Pacing

Choose how much decision time you get per tick (round):

| Mode | Time Per Tick | Best For |
|------|---------------|----------|
| **Deliberate** | 5 minutes | Learning and careful analysis |
| **Standard** | 60 seconds | Balanced gameplay |
| **Fast** | 1.2 seconds | Quick runs and replays |

Click **Begin Challenge** to start.

---

## 5. Play the Simulation

Each scenario runs for **30 ticks**. During each tick you can:

### Adjust Your Price
Use the **price slider** to set your base price. This is your primary tool — every pricing decision affects demand, revenue, and how you compare to the competitor.

### Apply a Discount
Select a discount level (5%–25%) to reduce your effective price and boost demand. Your effective price is calculated as:

> **Effective Price** = (Base Price − Discount) + Bundle Premium

### Launch a Promotion
Activate a time-limited marketing campaign to temporarily multiply demand:

| Promotion | Demand Boost | Duration | Cost |
|-----------|-------------|----------|------|
| Social Media Blast | 1.30× | 3 ticks | $50 |
| Email Campaign | 1.20× | 5 ticks | $80 |
| Influencer Partnership | 1.50× | 2 ticks | $150 |
| Loyalty Reward | 1.15× | 6 ticks | $60 |

Only one promotion can be active at a time. Promotion costs are deducted from your total revenue.

### Activate a Bundle
Each scenario has a unique **bundle** — an add-on that increases the effective price (premium) while also boosting demand. Toggle it on or off at any time.

---

## 6. Monitor the Dashboard

While the simulation is running, the gameplay screen displays real-time metrics:

- **Revenue** — Your cumulative total revenue.
- **Inventory** — Remaining stock.
- **Demand** — Current demand level based on your pricing, promotions, and competitor activity.
- **Competitor Price** — The competitor's current price (follows a scripted trajectory unique to each scenario).
- **Sentiment** — A customer sentiment indicator reflecting how your price compares to the base price.
- **Charts** — Visual trends for revenue, demand, and competitor price over time.

Use this information to adapt your strategy as the simulation progresses.

---

## 7. Review Your Results

After 30 ticks (or when inventory runs out), you'll see the **Results** screen:

- **Pricing Efficiency** — Your revenue as a percentage of the scenario's optimal revenue.
- **Letter Grade** — Based on efficiency:

| Grade | Efficiency | Pass/Fail |
|-------|-----------|-----------|
| A+ | ≥ 90% | Pass |
| A | ≥ 80% | Pass |
| B+ | ≥ 70% | Pass |
| B | ≥ 60% | Pass (unlocks next scenario) |
| C | ≥ 45% | Fail |
| D | < 45% | Fail |

- **AI Strategy Feedback** — Personalized analysis of your pricing decisions.

You can **retry** any scenario to improve your grade.

## Strategy Tips

- **High elasticity** (e.g., E-Commerce at 1.4) means customers are very price-sensitive. Small price changes cause large demand swings.
- **Low elasticity** (e.g., Hotel at 0.9) means demand is more stable. You can price higher without losing as many customers.
- **Don't just maximize units sold** — optimize for *revenue*. Selling everything at rock-bottom prices leaves money on the table.
- **Watch the competitor** every few ticks. Pricing below them boosts your demand; pricing far above them costs you customers.
- **Use promotions as timed boosts**, not permanent crutches. Time them around peak demand (mid-scenario) for maximum impact.
- **Manage inventory carefully.** Selling out too early means you miss high-price opportunities later. Holding too much stock means unsold inventory at the end.
- **Bundles are low-risk.** They add a price premium while also increasing demand — generally worth activating.

## Key Concepts

| Concept | What It Means |
|---------|---------------|
| **Dynamic Pricing** | Adjusting prices in real time based on demand, competition, and market conditions. |
| **Price Elasticity** | How much demand changes when price changes. Higher elasticity = more sensitive demand. |
| **Revenue Optimization** | Finding the price that maximizes total revenue, not just units sold or highest price. |
| **Competitive Factor** | Your demand is influenced by how your price compares to the competitor's price. |
| **Time Factor** | Demand naturally fluctuates during the simulation (peaks around tick 15). |
