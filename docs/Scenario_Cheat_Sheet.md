# Scenario Cheat Sheet — Grade A (≥ 80% efficiency)

**Grading:** A+ = 90%+, A = 80%+, B+ = 70%+, B = 60%+ (pass)

## Key Formulas

- **Demand** = `demandBase × (basePrice / effectivePrice)^elasticity × timeFactor × promoMultiplier × competitiveFactor`
- **Time factor** peaks mid-scenario (sine wave, up to 1.3× at tick 15)
- **Competitive factor** = `1 + (compPrice − yourPrice) / compPrice × 0.5` — pricing below the competitor boosts demand up to 1.5×

## 🛒 Scenario 1: E-Commerce — Target ≥ $14,400

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Optimal | $18,000 · Elasticity 1.4 · 200 units · Base $79 |
| Competitor | Starts $75, drops to $55, spikes to $90 mid-sale |

**Strategy:**
- **Ticks 1–14:** Set price **$65–70** (slightly below competitor who drops from $75→$61). The high elasticity rewards undercutting.
- **Ticks 14–16:** Competitor spikes to $85–90. **Raise to $80–85** to capture revenue while still underpricing them.
- **Ticks 17–30:** Competitor falls from $81→$55. **Follow down to $60–65**, stay at or slightly below them.
- **Bundle:** Activate Accessory Pack (+$15 premium, +10% demand) in the midgame spike when demand is high.
- **Promo:** Fire **Influencer Partnership** (1.5× demand, 2 ticks, $150) around ticks 13–16 when time factor peaks and competitor prices are high.

## ✈️ Scenario 2: Airline Seats — Target ≥ $30,400

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Optimal | $38,000 · Elasticity 1.1 · 90 seats · Base $180 |
| Competitor | Starts $170, climbs steadily to $350 |

**Strategy:**
- **Ticks 1–10:** Price **$160–175**, slightly below the competitor ($170→$235). Moderate elasticity means you can sell steadily.
- **Ticks 11–20:** Competitor climbs to $250–$307. **Raise to $200–260**, staying ~$40–50 below them to keep the competitive boost.
- **Ticks 21–30:** Competitor plateaus at $350. **Push to $280–330**. Scarcity drives urgency.
- **Bundle:** Activate Checked Bag Bundle (+$35, +8% demand) early — airlines sell ancillaries from the start.
- **Promo:** Use **Email Campaign** (1.2×, 5 ticks, $80) around ticks 12–16 for sustained demand during the time-factor peak.
- **Key:** Don't sell out too early. You want seats available for premium late-round pricing.

## 🏨 Scenario 3: Hotel — Target ≥ $20,000

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Optimal | $25,000 · Elasticity 0.9 · 60 rooms · Base $180 |
| Competitor | Holds $175 for 15 ticks, then climbs to $280 |

**Strategy:**
- **Ticks 1–14:** Competitor holds $175. Price at **$185–200** — low elasticity means demand barely drops at higher prices, and you capture premium revenue.
- **Ticks 15–30:** Competitor ramps from $182→$280. **Match the climb: $210→$280**. Stay slightly below them for the competitive boost.
- **Bundle:** Activate Breakfast Included (+$25, +12% demand) throughout — the 12% demand boost is the highest of any bundle.
- **Promo:** Fire **Influencer Partnership** around ticks 13–16 (peak time factor) to sell rooms at premium prices.
- **Key:** Low elasticity is your friend. Price aggressively — this isn't a volume game.

## 🎵 Scenario 4: Event Tickets — Target ≥ $45,600

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Optimal | $57,000 · Elasticity 1.6 · 500 tickets · Base $120 |
| Competitor | Drops $110→$70 (ticks 1–10), surges to $200 (ticks 20–25), falls to $130 |

**Strategy:**
- **Ticks 1–9:** Competitor drops to $70. **Price $85–95** — customers are extremely price-sensitive, so stay competitive. Volume is king with 500 tickets.
- **Ticks 10–20:** Competitor reverses and climbs to $157. **Gradually raise to $110–130**, staying below them.
- **Ticks 21–25:** Competitor peaks at $200. **Push to $140–160** — undercut them significantly for the competitive boost, but capture higher per-ticket revenue.
- **Ticks 26–30:** Competitor drops to $130. **Drop to $100–110** to move remaining inventory.
- **Bundle:** VIP Experience (+$40, +5% demand) — only activate when your base price is low enough that the $40 premium doesn't kill demand.
- **Promo:** Use **Social Media Blast** (1.3×, 3 ticks, $50) in ticks 13–16 for cheap demand during peak time factor. Save **Influencer Partnership** for the high-price window (ticks 21–24).
- **Key:** With 1.6 elasticity, never price too far above the competitor. Volume × moderate price > low volume × high price.

## General Tips

- **Time factor** peaks at tick 15 (1.3× demand) — align your best promos and highest prices here.
- **Stay below the competitor** for the competitive demand boost (up to 1.5×).
- **Only one promo at a time** — sequence them, don't waste overlaps.
- **Watch inventory** — if burning too fast, raise price; if too slow, drop price or run a promo.
